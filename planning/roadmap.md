# Learning and work roadmap

**Status:** Working sequence  
**Last reviewed:** 19 November 2026

This roadmap sequences learning and repository work. It is separate from [MVP scope and boundaries](mvp-scope.md), which defines what the repository is for.

## Phase 0 — Establish the repository foundation

**Status: Complete**

- Create the repository structure and define how it relates to the AI Services Portfolio and solution repositories.
- Record primary sources, source types, scope limits, and source-review conventions.
- Draft initial NIST and EU AI Act orientation pages, the EU evaluation matrix, and the screening record template.
- Add working status, roadmap, documentation conventions, and a source-record template.

**Checkpoint:** Initial documents and clear boundaries are available for review.

## Phase 1 — Validate the screening record and EU matrix

**Status: Planned — next**

1. Review each template section against the relevant primary source or authoritative guidance.
2. Record sources, exact relevant provisions, review dates, scope, limitations, and open questions using the source-record template.
3. Label each prompt as a legal requirement, official guidance, framework suggestion, separate legal check, or repository-authored working question.
4. Revise or remove prompts that are unsupported, too broad, or misleading; add precise citations where useful.
5. Update the status page to reflect what was reviewed and what remains uncertain.

**Checkpoint:** A source-mapped draft with unresolved questions visible; not a claim of legal validation.

## Phase 2 — Review selected examples

**Status: Planned**

- Review a small number of NIST AI RMF use-cases as reported applications.
- Review a small number of European Commission AI literacy practices for Article 4 learning; do not treat these as general AI Act project-evaluation cases.
- Record context, source claim, actions/evidence described, what the example does not establish, and our interpretation.
- Decide whether the material improves framework notes, reusable methods, or the screening template.

**Checkpoint:** Short evidence notes grounded in sources the project owner has reviewed.

## Phase 3 — Apply the reviewed method to a bounded scenario

**Status: Planned**

- Choose a bounded portfolio scenario after Phases 1–2.
- Apply the source-mapped screening method and distinguish assumptions from project evidence.
- Keep any live project-specific assessment in that solution’s repository; this repository may host a generalized or comparative learning use-case.
- Document gaps, controls, evaluation, human review, data/privacy/security considerations, and status only to the extent supported by evidence.

**Checkpoint:** A reviewable governance use-case that demonstrates method and limitations without claiming legal certification.

## Phase 4 — Expand based on demonstrated need

**Status: Later**

Possible work includes a NIST/EU comparison on one scenario, a deeper data governance and privacy module, and North American materials. Add these only when they serve the learning plan or a concrete portfolio need.

## Updating the roadmap

Mark work complete only when its stated checkpoint is met. Record substantive changes in the repository status page and preserve project-specific evidence in the relevant project repository.
