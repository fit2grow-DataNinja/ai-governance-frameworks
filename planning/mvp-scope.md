# MVP scope and boundaries

**Status:** Initial repository plan  
**Last reviewed:** 19 November 2026

## Purpose

Build a practical AI governance knowledge base and portfolio that shows how framework knowledge can inform project evaluation and governance work. Start with NIST AI RMF and the EU AI Act. Expand only when the MVP documents have been used and reviewed.

## MVP contents

1. Repository README and source-handling approach.
2. A source register identifying primary sources, versions/dates, and their role.
3. Concise framework orientation pages for NIST AI RMF and the EU AI Act.
4. A reusable EU AI Act project-evaluation matrix.
5. A reusable project-screening record.
6. A use-cases area reserved for future governance use-cases.

## What belongs where

| Material | Repository |
|---|---|
| Framework explanations, interpretations, reusable methods and templates | This repository |
| Completed assessment, project-specific risks, controls, test results, evidence, and status | Relevant solution repository |
| Cross-project summary and navigation | AI Services Portfolio |

A future use-case here may demonstrate a method or compare frameworks using a clearly identified scenario. It should not duplicate a live project’s detailed governance record.

## MVP boundaries

- Focus on NIST AI RMF and the EU AI Act; other frameworks can be added later.
- Separate binding legal text from Commission explanations, voluntary framework guidance, and repository-authored interpretation.
- Do not claim templates are official forms unless linked to and accurately identified as such.
- Do not infer that a portfolio prototype is high-risk or legally compliant without a scoped assessment.
- Distinguish implemented, planned, not addressed, and not applicable.

## Later candidates (not part of the MVP)

Framework crosswalks; additional privacy-law and North American materials; vendor review, release-readiness, incident-response, and monitoring use-cases; more detailed control libraries and evidence examples.

## Change rule

Add a document when it supports a defined learning or portfolio need. Record its source, purpose, status, and review date. Avoid expanding the directory structure just to reserve speculative space.
