## What the source says

Annex IV sets out information to be included in technical documentation for high-risk AI systems to which Article 11 applies. The required documentation is linked to the applicable legal obligations and system facts.

## Evidence and limits

- Actions, controls, or evidence described: Documentation topics prescribed for applicable high-risk systems.
- What the source does not establish: That every AI project must create an Annex IV file or that a generic checklist satisfies all obligations.
- Scope, assumptions, or limitations: Read with Article 11 and other applicable provisions in the current consolidated Regulation.
- Validation/endorsement: Annex IV is part of the binding Regulation; the Service Desk page is a presentation of that text.
