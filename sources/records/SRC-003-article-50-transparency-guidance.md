## What the source says

The Commission guidance explains Article 50 transparency obligations, including disclosure for direct AI interaction, marking/detectability of certain synthetic outputs, and disclosure in specified emotion-recognition, biometric-categorisation, deepfake, and public-interest text cases. The Regulation sets the legal duties and exceptions; the guidance supports interpretation.

## Evidence and limits

- Actions, controls, or evidence described: Guidance for interpreting transparency obligations and implementation.
- What the source does not establish: That any particular project triggers a duty or that a proposed notice/marking is legally sufficient.
- Scope, assumptions, or limitations: Apply the exact Article 50 paragraph, exception, system facts, role, and transition.
- Validation/endorsement: Official Commission interpretive guidance, not binding legislation.
