## What the source says

The pathway describes a toolkit for dataset transparency documentation. It asks users to define transparency with their organization and stakeholders and co-create a schema that records human decisions; refine and validate the schema against real-world datasets and feedback; produce Data Cards for readers with different backgrounds; and audit the cards using structured assessments and user studies.

## Evidence and limits

- Actions, controls, or evidence described by the source: Suggested workflow and learning materials for producing and auditing dataset documentation.
- What the source does not establish: It does not show that Google or another organization used the toolkit for a particular dataset, nor that a Data Card provides complete dataset provenance or sufficient risk evidence.
- Scope, assumptions, or limitations: This is a public training resource/toolkit, not an internal policy, completed dataset record, or NIST Profile. The detailed codelabs may include additional instructions beyond the overview page reviewed.
- Does the source state whether the example is validated, endorsed, or illustrative? It is presented as a toolkit and learning pathway; this review did not validate outcomes from its use.

## Our analysis

- Possible relevance to a project or reusable document: Possible method-level links to MAP 2.3 (dataset selection/suitability documentation) and, where a project actually engages external actors and integrates their feedback, GOVERN 5.1 or 5.2.
- Adaptations required and rationale: Do not map the toolkit’s existence as evidence of a control in a company. For a project, inspect the actual Data Card, schema, feedback record, and validation evidence before mapping to NIST. The external-feedback NIST links remain conditional.
- Open questions: Which detailed fields are included in the Data Cards schema and how the auditing method measures the card’s usefulness.
- Confidence: High that the page describes the stated workflow; low-to-medium for any outcome mapping without reviewing a concrete Data Card and its use.
