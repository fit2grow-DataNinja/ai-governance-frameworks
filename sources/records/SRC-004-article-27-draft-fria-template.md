## What the source says

The Commission announced draft questionnaire/template material for the Article 27(5) FRIA. The consultation was still open on the review date. Article 27 applies to specified deployers and high-risk use contexts; it is not a universal assessment requirement for every AI project. The Regulation allows certain information to be cross-referenced with a GDPR data protection impact assessment when its conditions are met.

## Evidence and limits

- Actions, controls, or evidence described: Draft questionnaire structure to support an Article 27 assessment.
- What the source does not establish: An adopted official template, legal compliance, or that Article 27 applies to a particular deployer.
- Scope, assumptions, or limitations: Draft may change; determine statutory applicability from Article 27 and deployment facts.
- Validation/endorsement: Commission consultation draft; not adopted as of 23 November 2026.
