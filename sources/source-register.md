# Source register

**Last reviewed:** 19 November 2026  
This register prioritizes primary sources. The “Use” column describes how this repository uses each source; it does not imply endorsement or complete legal analysis.

| Source | Type / status | Used for | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| [NIST AI RMF 1.0](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework) | Official voluntary framework | NIST framework overview and outcome structure | NIST’s Resource Center states AI RMF 1.0 is being revised. Check for a newer version before relying on this snapshot. |
| [NIST AI RMF Playbook](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/playbook/) | Official companion resource; suggested actions are voluntary | Operational examples and documentation practices | Not a checklist or prescribed ordered process; select and adapt relevant suggestions. |
| [NIST AI RMF Generative AI Profile, NIST AI 600-1](https://www.nist.gov/publications/artificial-intelligence-risk-management-framework-generative-artificial-intelligence) | Official NIST profile | GenAI-specific risks and suggested actions | Companion to AI RMF 1.0; confirm latest NIST status when updating. |
| [NIST AI RMF use cases](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/usecases/) | NIST-published examples | Examples of organizations applying the RMF | NIST says it does not validate or endorse the individual approaches shown. |
| [EU AI Act, consolidated text](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:02024R1689-20260727) | Binding EU regulation (consolidated text) | Legal references and obligations | Consolidated text identified as of 27 July 2026. Check EUR-Lex for later amendments and applicable dates. |

| [AI Act Annex IV](https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/en/ai-act/annex-4) | Annex to binding Regulation | Technical-documentation elements for applicable high-risk systems | Not a universal template for all AI projects. |

| [IAPP AIGP certification page](https://iapp.org/certify/aigp) | Certification scope and study resources | Learning alignment | The current Body of Knowledge and exam blueprint are the authority for exam scope. |

## Source-use conventions

- Cite the exact article, annex, framework function/category, publication, or version where possible.
- Label legal text, regulator/Commission guidance, voluntary framework material, and repository interpretation separately.
- Record the date a source was checked when updating a document.
- A link in this register is a research pointer, not proof that a particular legal obligation applies to a project.
